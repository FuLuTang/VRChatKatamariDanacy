## 逐字摘录

> **KatamariBall** (スポーツ塊, _Sports Katamari_) is a variant of Versus Mode in _Once Upon A Katamari_. The goal of this mode is to get the most points at the end of the match. **Points are obtained by depositing objects into the S.S. Prince, which moves to a different location every 60 seconds. Doing so shrinks the katamari, leaving the player vulnerable to getting rolled up (which respawns the player instead of making them escape by wiggling the analog sticks). At the end of the match, bonus points are awarded by the King based on merits such as rolling up the most objects or having the largest katamari.** A selection of items are available to use to either help you or attack others.
